How to remove an image background for free

Six steps. Your photo never needs a cloud detour.

You get the speed of automatic background removal with the final say of a hands-on photo editor.

  1. 01

    Choose a photo

    Open your image in Subra AI. The photo stays on the device while the on-device model separates the subject from its background.

  2. 02

    Inspect the cutout

    Use the split comparison to move between the original and the result. Check hair, clothing, hands, product corners, and other difficult boundaries.

  3. 03

    Refine the mask

    Erase unwanted areas or restore parts of the subject. Choose the brush shape and hardness that suit the correction you need.

  4. 04

    Tune the edges

    Adjust mask threshold, softness, cleanup, refinement, and color-spill removal—or start with Natural, Sharp, or Hair Detail.

  5. 05

    Choose the new background

    Keep it transparent, make it white, select a color, or place the subject over another image you choose.

  6. 06

    Export and save

    Review the final composition, then export it from the app. No personal-image upload is required for the editing workflow.

Frequently asked questions

Everything to know before your first cutout.

How can I remove an image background for free?

Download Subra AI, open the background-removal tool, choose a photo, review the automatically created mask, refine it if needed, choose a transparent, white, color, or image background, and export the result. The feature is designed to run locally on your device.

Can I remove a photo background without uploading it online?

Yes. Subra AI performs this background-removal workflow on device, so your source image does not need to be sent to a cloud AI service. Download the app and required model resources before working offline.

Can I change the background of a photo to white?

Yes. After separating the subject, choose white as the replacement background. This is useful for profile pictures, marketplace listings, catalog images, documents, and clean product shots.

Can I make the background transparent?

Yes. You can keep the extracted subject on a transparent background and export the result for designs, presentations, product listings, thumbnails, or later editing.

Can I replace the background with another picture or color?

Yes. Subra AI supports a transparent result, a plain white or custom-color background, and a replacement image selected by you.

What if the automatic background removal misses hair or small details?

Use mask refinement to erase or restore regions, then adjust threshold, edge softness, edge cleanup, edge refinement, and color-spill removal. Natural, Sharp, and Hair Detail presets provide useful starting points.

Does Subra AI work without internet?

The editing workflow runs on device and can work offline after the app and any required model resources are already downloaded. You do not need an active connection to send each personal photo to a server.

Is Subra AI useful for founders and small businesses?

It can help create consistent profile images, product cutouts, listing photos, pitch-deck assets, social posts, and marketing compositions on a phone—without adding a per-image cloud workflow or exposing customer and product imagery to a third-party image service.

Can I remove backgrounds from multiple images at once?

This page does not promise bulk processing. Subra AI currently focuses on a controlled, image-by-image mobile workflow with visual review, mask refinement, edge adjustment, and export.

Will the exported image always be HD quality?

Output quality depends on the source image, subject complexity, device resources, mask quality, and export settings. Start with a clear, well-lit image and inspect detailed edges before saving.
