Meet Subra AI Hydration Coach

Subra AI gives Water Tracker a more flexible layer of guidance, helping users ask real hydration questions, get concise coaching, and go beyond fixed screens when they want more personalized insight.

Powered by Gemma 4On-device privacyHydration coaching
More flexible than fixed screens: users can ask for custom hydration history or trend ranges that normal dashboards do not show by default.
Built for privacy by default: after setup, Subra AI is designed to work on-device instead of sending personal hydration chats to cloud servers.
Practical daily coaching: reminder ideas, hydration habit suggestions, and intake guidance stay short, useful, and action-oriented.
Real setup detail: Subra AI requires a one-time brain download of about 2.5 GB, then it can be exported, stored, and reused without repeating the same large download.
